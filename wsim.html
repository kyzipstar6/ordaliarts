Climate description

This coastal Pacific–Southern Hemisphere village experiences warm, humid conditions year-round. Being located at the southern hemisphere, summers are in the first months of the year and winters at the midst.

The average temperature is of 25°C, while the average humidity is of 80%. This means a rather hot and humid climate.

Although not as rainy as other equatorial islands, here it rains often, being the yearly average of 1200 mm.

The island enjoys of low speed NW winds year round, though it could experience tropical storms.
