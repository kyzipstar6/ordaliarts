🌴 Climate Description

This coastal Pacific–Southern Hemisphere village experiences warm, humid conditions year-round. Summers occur early in the year (January–March), and winters mid-year (June–August).

The average temperature is around 25 °C, and humidity averages 80%, creating a hot and sticky climate.

Rain is frequent but moderate, with an annual average of 1200 mm. Gentle NW trade winds are common, but tropical storms can sometimes occur.
