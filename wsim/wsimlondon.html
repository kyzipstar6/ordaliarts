🏙 Climate Description of London

London features a relatively mild climate compared to other cities at similar latitudes. Its weather reputation is often gloomy, but in reality, the Atlantic Ocean moderates the climate significantly.

The Atlantic helps protect the city from cold continental air masses between November and April. The average annual temperature is about 8 °C, and the average humidity hovers around 80%, making it cool and damp.

London receives approximately 680 mm of precipitation annually, fairly evenly distributed across the year.

Being close to the ocean, the city experiences frequent winds, usually coming from the west and contributing to the overall humidity and mildness.
