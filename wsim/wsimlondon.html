Climate description

London features a relativelly warm climate if we take into consideration other cities aituated at the same latitude but diferent longitude.

However, its reputation of having a bad weather often darkens the perception of the city.

The fact is that the Atlantic quite softens the climatey protectong the city against the cold european air in the months from November to April. The average temperature is of 8°C, while the average humidity is of 80%. This means a rather cool and humid climate.

The yearly precipitation is of 680 mm.

Being located near the Atlantic Ocean, the city features a rather windy climate with cool, humid winds from West direction.
