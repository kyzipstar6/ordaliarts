🌴 Climate Description

The climate in Colorado is of the type Bsk (cold semi-arid), translating in the case of Columbine in an yearly precipitation of about 500 mm.

The value of the anual average temperature (10.6°C) is not as important than another places, for there is big amplitude between day and night. In fact, the capital of Colorado averages 17°C of amplitude in some months.

The summer has hot days, several times yearly over 35°C, while winter has its chilly weather, with january averaging -7°C low. Wind is common, because of the location in the inner continent. Tornadoes can be in Colorado seen.
