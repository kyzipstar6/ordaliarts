🌇 Climate Description of Barcelona

Barcelona has a Mediterranean climate (Köppen Csa), with mild winters and hot summers. The average annual temperature is around 15 °C with moderate rainfall.

The city enjoys at least 140 hours of sunshine each month. The cloudiest periods are typically in late autumn and early spring.

In recent years, Barcelona has experienced rising temperatures. Rainfall has become less frequent but more intense, often causing flash floods. The last snowfall was in 2018. Previously, snow would fall roughly every two years.

The city receives approximately 650 mm of rainfall per year, mostly concentrated in the fall months.
