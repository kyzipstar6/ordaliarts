Climate description

The climate of the city is of the type mediterranean Csa, with average temperatures of 15•C and moderate rainfall.

The city enjoys of at least 140 montly sunshine hours each month. The cloudiest months are those of the late autnum and those of the early spring.

The warm weather of Barcelona has experienced an increase of temperatures the last years. Rains have become less frequent and more harmful due to the quantity of precipitation fallen at once. The last time it snowed at the city was in 2018. Before then it used to snow one each two years. The mean anual precipitation quantity is of roughly 650 mm, spread all over the year, with tendency to occur in the months of the fall.
