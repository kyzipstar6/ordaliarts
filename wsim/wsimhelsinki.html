🌴 Climate Description

Helsinki's climate classification is Köppen Dfb, which features a humid continental climate description.

Just as what happens in other atlantic cities such as London or Paris, the city enjoys the temperating influence of the Atlantic Ocean, that thus keeps temperatures protected from the freezing artic winds in winter time.

If we go on with comparison with other nortern cities we will notice that this swedish city enjoys of quantious sunshine in summer (300 hours vs. 200 hors of London).

However, the city is colder than London due to its proximity to the European continent and more vulnerable to the cold eastern winds bringing the freezing siberian influence.

That is what makes the 🌡️ temperatures average -2°C in the winter months, but allows for as well the sun to shine. Hence July can average 18°C with temperatures achieving often 30°C, which is really noteworthy for a city located 60°N.

The precipitations are well spread all over the year, a typical behavior of atlantic located cities. It falls 650 mm of precipitation throughout the year.
