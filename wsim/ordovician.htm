Early Ordovician Period (485-470 million years ago)

The Early Ordovician was characterized by warm, tropical climates with high sea levels. Atmospheric CO₂ levels were extremely high compared to today, leading to a greenhouse Earth. Shallow seas covered much of the continents, creating extensive marine habitats for the diverse invertebrate life that flourished during this time.
